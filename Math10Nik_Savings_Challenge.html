STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION

1. Given: P = ₱18,000; r = 0.08; n = 4; t = 2 years

2. Formula for future value:

FV = P(1 + r/n)nt

3. Substitute:

FV = 18,000(1 + 0.08/4)4 × 2

4. Simplify:

FV = 18,000(1 + 0.02)8
FV = 18,000(1.02)8
FV = 18,000(1.171659381...)

5. Future value:

FV = ₱21,089.87

6. Compound interest:

CI = FV − P
CI = 21,089.87 − 18,000
CI = ₱3,089.87
After two years, the student's ₱18,000 deposit grows to ₱21,089.87, earning ₱3,089.87 in compound interest.
